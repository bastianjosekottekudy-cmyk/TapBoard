## Short description

Bluetooth keyboard and mouse remote for your PC, Chromebook, and more.

## Full description

TapBoard turns your Android phone into a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse.

Pair with Windows, macOS, Linux, Chromebooks, and many smart TVs that accept Bluetooth keyboards — no PC software required.

Built for real use
• Multi-touch touchpad with scroll and right-click
• Full on-screen keyboard with modifiers and function keys
• Adjustable sensitivity and haptic feedback

Privacy-first
No account. No ads. No cloud telemetry. Bluetooth stays on your devices.

Requires Android 9+. Works best on phones whose Bluetooth stack supports acting as a HID keyboard/mouse (Pixel, many Samsung devices, and others).
