Privacy Policy — TapBoard

Last updated: 2026-08-03

TapBoard is a local Bluetooth remote-input utility. This policy describes what the Android app processes on your device.

Summary

  • No accounts.
  • No cloud sync or analytics by default.
  • Bluetooth traffic stays between your phone and the paired host.

Data we process

  • Bluetooth device names and addresses of hosts you choose to connect to (for pairing/connection UI only; not uploaded).
  • App preferences (sensitivity, theme, onboarding flag) in private app storage.

Not collected

Personal identity, contacts, photos, location history, advertising IDs, or crash telemetry in v1.

Permissions (Android)

PermissionWhy
Bluetooth / Nearby devicesAct as a Bluetooth HID keyboard and mouse
Location (older Android)Required by the OS for Bluetooth scanning on API ≤30
Foreground service / notificationsKeep the HID session alive while you use the remote
VibrateOptional haptic feedback on clicks

Children’s privacy

TapBoard is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Open an issue in the project repository or contact the publisher email listed on the Google Play store listing.
